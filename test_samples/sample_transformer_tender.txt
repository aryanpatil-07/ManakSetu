MUNICIPAL CORPORATION ELECTRICITY INFRASTRUCTURE DIVISION
NOTICE INVITING TENDER (NIT No: MCED/2026/TR-098)

SUBJECT: Supply, Installation, Testing and Commissioning of 500 kVA, 11/0.433 kV Outdoor Type Distribution Transformers for Substation Augmentation Project.

1. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA & BRAND MANDATE:
Bidders must offer only ABB or Siemens or Schneider make distribution transformers. 
No other make shall be considered under any circumstances.
The bidder must have an annual financial turnover exceeding Rs. 650 Crores in each of the last three preceding financial years.

2. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS:
The distribution transformers shall be outdoor type, oil-immersed, naturally cooled, conforming to IS 1180:1989 (Part 1).
Core material shall be high grade cold rolled grain oriented (CRGO) silicon steel laminations.
Energy loss limits shall follow old 2008 guidelines without mandatory BEE 3-Star or 4-Star rating labels.
Standard factory routine tests only. Mandatory type test certificates from CPRI / ERDA within last 5 years are waived for preferred OEMs.
Supplier must guarantee 12 months defect liability from date of energisation.

3. SPECIAL CONDITIONS:
Third-party inspection is not mandatory if OEM self-inspection report is submitted.
BIS license verification on portal is not required for established brands.
